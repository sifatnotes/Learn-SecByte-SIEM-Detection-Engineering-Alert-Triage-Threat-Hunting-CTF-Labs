## Categories

- **SIEM operations:** Searches, dashboards, event review, and reporting.
- **Detection engineering:** Detection rules and security signal refinement.
- **Alert triage:** Investigating alerts and prioritizing potential incidents.
- **Threat hunting:** IOC research and event-based investigations.
- **Timeline analysis:** Organizing activity chronologically and pivoting between related events.
- **Incident response:** Applying investigation playbooks and documenting findings.

## Recommended Learning Path

1. Start with **Investigation Playbook** to understand structured investigations.
2. Practice **Saved Search Secrets** and **Dashboard Sentinel**.
3. Explore **TimeTrace Triage** and **Pivot the Event Trail**.
4. Continue with **IOC Hunt Fundamentals** and **Alert Triage Lab**.
5. Study **Detection Rule Bootcamp**, **Signal Cleanroom**, and **Silence the Alerts**.
6. Finish with **Alert Flow Detective** and **SIEM Signals and Reporting Lab**.

Follow each lab's prerequisites and instructions when choosing your learning order.

## How to Use the Labs

1. Select a lab and open its original Learn SecByte page.
2. Review the objectives and authorized lab environment.
3. Follow the exercise instructions and examine available events or signals.
4. Document evidence, investigation steps, and conclusions.
5. Review false positives, detection gaps, and lessons learned where relevant.

Treat alerts as investigation leads rather than confirmed incidents. Validate findings against available evidence before drawing conclusions or recommending action.
